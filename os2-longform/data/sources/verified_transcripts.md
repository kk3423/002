## A1. NHTSA — "Takata Air Bag Recall" (2023-10-25) · videoId y2yJ27ROjVg · AGENCY (primary)
There are millions of defective Takata air bags on the road that need repair. So why are some airbags causing deaths and injuries in crashes? The problem is with a chemical inside the airbag that works to inflate the airbag during a crash. In a crash, a recalled Takata airbag can explode, sending sharp metal fragments flying at 200 miles per hour. This can cause serious injury or even death. The greatest risk is in older vehicles, especially vehicles driven in hot, humid areas. So how can you find out if your vehicle has a recalled airbag? Download the Safer Car app to get notified of vehicle safety recalls, or visit NHTSA.gov/recalls and enter your vehicle identification number, known as the VIN, and sign up for recall alerts. If you have a recall, contact your local dealer to get it fixed for free. Make sure the airbag in your vehicle is there to save your life, not take it. At NHTSA, we believe safe cars save lives. Check for recalls now on the safer car app, or at NHTSA.gov/recalls.

## A4. NHTSA — "Vehicle Safety Defects" (2023-10-25) · SXyJEkch24c · AGENCY (primary)
Hearing from you is an important part of NHTSA's vehicle defect investigation process. NHTSA investigates your safety-related vehicle complaints, and identifies safety defects that could cause death or injury to you or your family. A safety defect is a problem with your vehicle that poses an unreasonable risk to your safety that could result in a crash and injury or death. This can be a problem with the vehicle's materials, how the vehicle's built, or how it performs. Safety defects can include many types of things, including brake failure, steering failure, or exploding airbags. Things like peeling paint or problems with your radio are not usually safety defects. Do you think your car has a safety defect? Visit NHTSA.gov/recalls to report. We review every complaint we receive. At NHTSA, we believe safe cars save lives. Visit NHTSA.gov/recalls for more information.
