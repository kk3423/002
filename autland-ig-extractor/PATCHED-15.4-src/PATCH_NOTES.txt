Fixes
- Comment resume: the history counted listed commenters as extracted, so a
  resume after a 429 skipped commenters never checked (98 of 150 in the
  simulation; all of them when the 429 came on the first check). Counts now
  use checked profiles only, the end of the comment list no longer marks the
